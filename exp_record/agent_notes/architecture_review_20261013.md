# Architecture review tied to precision completion — 13 Oct 2026

Requested alongside the authorized R5 objective experiment. This is a code/evidence review, **not a second experiment registration**. No architecture, task success predicate or repair is changed during the active run. The goal remains measurable safe completion in precision/contact manipulation, rather than classification scores or avoiding all contact.

## Findings from the implemented interfaces

1. `models/nets.py:OrbiSimDynamics.rollout` predicts a state sequence, but its risk head deliberately ignores that sequence: each candidate action is paired with the same measured current context. Consequently a max-over-horizon gate is invariant to action permutation. This is a genuine structural limitation for “brake before contact” versus “accelerate into contact.” The code's historical comment calling this the correct physical model should not be read as a demonstrated validity claim for future contact evolution. Earlier synthetic probes establish the limitation; earlier GRU results do **not** establish that merely adding recurrence solves it.
2. The previous `models/sequence_risk.py` GRU evolves a latent over **future candidate actions**, initialized from the current and previous observation and last action. It is not an estimator over a history of measured contacts/responses. The static and GRU models both had severely limited original-proposal event support. Order sensitivity and hidden-dynamics observability remain different questions.
3. The deployed `obs` in `sims/torch_push.py` contains goal-relative position, object velocity, pusher-relative position, time and square-symmetric yaw/angular velocity. It has no force-sensor history. Allowed measured motion responses can inform a contact belief, but hidden mass/friction and simulator regime tags cannot silently become its runtime inputs. A real sensor addition would change information access and require matched baselines; it is not an architecture-only improvement.
4. Current `scale_repair` changes the magnitude of the original proposal. It cannot express an independent lateral correction, retreat/re-contact or orientation adjustment. In this simulator the actor also has only2 planar velocity commands, not a manipulator's full6-DoF/gripper interface. Scaling may prevent overload without maintaining alignment/progress. However the existing non-collinear study is parked; expressiveness is a limitation, not proof that a particular larger action family helps.
5. A short safe chunk does not imply that resuming the frozen policy will finish. The transition study's pause arm is direct evidence: locally smaller jumps did not improve overall safe completion. The prior learned progress-ranking arm also failed to demonstrate net benefit; adding an unvalidated distance term to the same inaccurate predicted states is not a substantiated remedy.
6. **The current task metric is not yet a strong precision metric.** `PushSim.success` checks goal distance<.02m and translational speed<.05m/s. Although the environment has a6mm wall clearance, the success predicate does not directly require yaw tolerance, insertion depth, a stable dwell interval or low angular speed. Thus a safe-success gain here is a mechanism result; it cannot by itself substantiate fine placement/alignment capability. Do not reinterpret6mm geometry as a6mm measured success requirement. Keep the registered metric unchanged and freeze physically meaningful precision criteria before the eventual single-task transfer.

## Ranked recommendations and evidence needed

### 1. Separate intervention decision from continuous repair scoring — active now

Use an event-oriented short-horizon gate for “should intervene?” and a separate continuous physical-risk evaluator for action repair. Event probability units should not be inserted into the old `.8 / .75×score` repair target, since that changes the meaning of the intervention and can saturate useful gradients. The current `GateRepairVerifier` interface and the R5 experiment implement this separation while freezing repair. This is the least confounded next algorithm test and it directly follows the strong GT-gate substitution evidence.

Promotion needs event-vs-matched-regression **closed-loop** gains across the fixed seeds, alongside policy-relative success/CVR/safe and rescue/harm. Event loss is only a testable component, not a claim that the dissertation's full differentiable-physics contribution has already been achieved.

### 2. If representation is implicated, use a compact contact-state predictor with explicit causal action evolution

A plausible later design is a small measured-history encoder plus a causal, action-conditioned contact latent: relative clearance, alignment, approach velocity and contact response should evolve with preceding candidate actions. Predict a physically meaningful short-term residual/contact event rather than blindly feeding a long drifting state rollout back into the old risk head. This addresses known contact timing/order deficiencies more directly than widening the MLP or rerunning the failed generic GRU.

This is **conditional**, not automatically queued. Before proposing it, use the current study to establish whether matched objective/data treatment leaves structured misses. A subsequent experiment must separate measured-history access from action-prefix representation and event objective, with matched inputs/data/parameter/query budgets and all seeds. Any H10 anticipation claim still needs valid new training support; the failed collectors cannot be waived. Additional history must be available at the existing physical-step rate to all comparator architectures and may not grant extra policy/action updates.

### 3. If detection becomes reliable but completion still fails, make repair task- and continuation-aware

For precision work, the relevant repair constraints concern contact force **and** reaching/staying in the allowed position/orientation/insertion region. A future bounded local repair could preserve a nominal endpoint/alignment objective while limiting predicted contact risk and action discontinuity. Its candidate proposal and acceptance must be evaluated under actual policy resumption, not judged only by H10 risk or local distance. A fixed-budget non-collinear family is one possible diagnostic tool, not a justified next launch today.

Promotion requires the observed failure to be a repair/continuation failure rather than missed detection, and improved safe completion over the fixed repair on identical gate decisions/candidate budgets. Prior progress and smoothing failures make this evidentiary requirement important. Exact continuation witnesses remain diagnosis-only; any learned completion/value head needs separately authorized fresh training data and matched information access.

## Precision-task acceptance design

On the eventual one everyday contact task, freeze feasible geometric tolerances (position/orientation/depth as applicable), stable completion duration and force/impact constraints from the simulator's actual mechanics before tuning. Report precision-conditioned success, SR/CVR/safe success and paired rescue/harm, not just “force lower.” A manipulator/controller action-convention check and a baseline able to reach the contact stage are prerequisites. Current full-7B planar-head experiments do not establish native OpenVLA manipulation-head compatibility.

**Decision order stays linear:** finish the bounded gate-objective experiment → decide whether that change improves safe completion → only then register the one next change supported by its failure pattern. Architecture suggestions do not reopen parallel SEAL, smoothing, scale or benchmark searches.
